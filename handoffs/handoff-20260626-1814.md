## PRIORITY 2 — deliverable B: the squad-form panel
Design agreed during the Phase-G/H grills (not yet built):
- Each team's **current squad** = players whose most-recent appearance was for that club. Primary home: the **fixture page** (both teams side by side); per CONTEXT.md "Squad form" + non-negotiable #5. May also sit on a team hub.
- **SOURCE = FBref appearances only.** Squad membership + transfers are **derived from `player_match.team_id` changes** over a player's date-ordered timeline. **No Transfermarkt, no roster scrape** (the user explicitly chose this; overturns ADR 0003's lean toward FBref *squad pages* for Job C).
- **This is where the deferred ADR belongs** — write it when building B. It must record: (a) derive-squad-from-appearances vs Transfermarkt/FBref-squad-page; (b) the **`current_team_id` fragility** — it's set "last-written" (`ingestion/players.py` `_upsert_player` on_conflict), NOT guaranteed chronologically-latest, so the squad panel should derive "current club" at query time as the team of the player's `MAX(date)` appearance, not trust the stored column; (c) the accepted **"ghost" edge case** — a player transferring OUT to an uncovered league never updates, lingering in the old squad (user will handle later).
- Open design Qs for B's grill: where it lives (fixture page vs team hub vs both), how much per-player detail (one headline metric vs expandable mini-table), and the ~25×2-players-per-fixture compute (consider the same raw-rows-client-aggregate pattern as ADR 0005).
