## The qualifier problem (next session's core)
- **Blocker:** soccerdata 1.9.0 `read_seasons` does `(html_table,) = tree.xpath("//table[@id='seasons']")` (fbref.py:214). FBref qualifier *history* pages (WC quals ×7 confeds + play-offs, Euros/AFCON/Asian qualifying) have **no `table#seasons`** → crash at season resolution, before any schedule fetch. Finals + NL pages have the table; that's why they worked.
- **The season links ARE in the page** (e.g. `/en/comps/6/<year>/...` for 1998–2022 on the UEFA WC-qual page, cached at `~/soccerdata/data/FBref/seasons_INT-World Cup Qualification UEFA.html`). A ~20-line shim (vendored `read_seasons` fallback or subclass) is feasible but **unproven** — the qualifier *schedule* page structure is untested. That's the PoC: shim + one qualifier competition-edition live, before committing to the full qualifier backfill (the larger half of ADR 0011's ~3,500–4,500 estimate).
- league_dict already has all 8 WC-qual entries + Euros/AFCON/Asian qualifying (synced globally). Qualifier editions to target live in `_intl_spike.py` CANDIDATES (untracked scratch; spike results in `_intl_spike_results.json`).
- Qualifier campaigns straddle the 6-season boundary — held partially by design (ADR 0011: dates rule).
