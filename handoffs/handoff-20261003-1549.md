## 2. Matchup view: grill first
- **The grill agenda and facts are in the previous handoff:**
  `handoffs/handoff-20261003-1422.md`, section "The Matchup view: grill first".
  - The agenda covers metric pairs, windows and venue, where the view sits, and any
    "combined" figure (it must not read as a prediction).
  - The key fact is that every `team_match` row pairs with its opponent's row, so
    "against" figures need a self-join and no ingestion.
- **New since then, and relevant to the grill:**
  - **The Fixture view is busier.** It now carries an Appointed-referee card under the
    heroes (`AppointedReferee` in `frontend/src/pages/FixtureView.tsx`). Decide where
    the Matchup sits relative to it and to the Team form / Head-to-Head / Squad form
    toggle.
  - **`FixtureRow` now carries `referee_id` / `referee`.** The planned follow-up, a
    "Referee" filter on team and player pages, is cheap now. Ask whether the Matchup
    should be able to filter by the appointed referee, or stay out of scope.
  - **Cards in a Matchup:** team rows follow football-data.co.uk, which drops both
    yellows on a two-yellow dismissal (689 of 707 league sides; ADR 0018). If the
    Matchup pairs "cards for" against "cards against", decide which counting rule it
    uses and say so on the page. Don't mix team-row and player-row cards silently.
